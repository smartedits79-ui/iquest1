How we decide a chapter is finished

One subject, one specialist

Your child is not handed to a single tutor for everything. Maths is taught by the maths teacher, science by the science teacher, English by the English teacher. Each one works in their own subject all day, every day.

Practice until it is automatic

Concepts are explained once and then drilled. Sums are worked in class, not just watched. A child who can only follow along has not learnt it yet. A child who can do it alone has.

Notes your child can revise from

Structured, chapter-wise notes prepared by the subject teacher, so revision at home in March is not a hunt through six months of half-finished rough work.

Revision batches before exams

Separate revision batches run ahead of school exams and boards, so the last few weeks are spent tightening weak chapters instead of racing through the syllabus.

We believe in practice-based learning.

A concept is explained once, then drilled until your child can do it without help. Watching someone solve it is not the same as solving it.
